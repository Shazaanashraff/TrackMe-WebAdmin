Two more directions

The first three leaned austere. These two go the other way — richer and more contemporary, while staying professional. Both still sit on shadcn + Tailwind, so the tokens, primitives and data layer already committed (CP 0.1–0.3) survive either choice. Same Dashboard screen as before, so it's a like-for-like comparison.

Throws out the "KPI row + table below" formula. Panel size encodes importance instead: the live map earns the biggest tile, the approval queue gets a tall one because it's the thing you actually act on, and the vanity numbers shrink to small tiles. Big radii and soft depth make it feel like a 2026 product rather than a bootstrap admin. The accent is amber — signal-yellow is native to transit, and it dodges the default SaaS emerald/indigo.

That one structural move plus a petrol-teal accent does most of the work: it stays calm and utterly conventional to operate, but it looks expensive. The safest of the five to build, because it's 01's bones with a better suit.
